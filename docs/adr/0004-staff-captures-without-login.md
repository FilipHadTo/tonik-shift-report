# Staff Members add Captures via a shared link, without login

One Closing Manager can't witness everything, so Staff Members (door, bar, security) can add Captures to tonight's Shift Report from their phones. They join through a per-Night link or QR and type their name and role once — no accounts. Their Captures are attributed and, like everything else, become Drafts the Closing Manager must confirm. We chose attribution over authentication to keep friction near zero for people with no stake in the report; whether that's enough to count as a witness statement is an open question for Dorota.
