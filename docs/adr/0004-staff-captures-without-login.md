# Staff Members add Captures via a shared link, without login

One Closing Manager can't witness everything, so Staff Members (door, bar, security) can add Captures to tonight's Shift Report from their phones. They join through the report's link or QR, shown by the Closing Manager from "Invite staff", and type their name and role once — no accounts. Their Captures are attributed and land in the Closing Manager's Inbox, and the Closing Manager decides what goes into the report (ADR-0007). We chose attribution over authentication to keep friction near zero for people with no stake in the report; Dorota confirmed (2026-10-29) that the minimum for an Incident is time, date, place, type and description, so witness statements are out of scope.
